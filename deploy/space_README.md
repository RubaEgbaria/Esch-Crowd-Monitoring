# Crowd Monitoring: LiDAR + Camera Fusion

AE3S Lab, University of Luxembourg.

People detection and counting with a Blickfeld Qb2 LiDAR, a camera (YOLO11m) and their
fusion (Stack-GBT). Open the app, enter the shared password, open the **baseline** run
and click **Live view**.

Source and documentation: https://github.com/RubaEgbaria/Esch-Crowd-Monitoring (branch `web-app`).
